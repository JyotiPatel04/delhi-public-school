World-Class School Facilities

A secure, digitally enabled, and nurturing environment crafted for Classes 1 to 10 in Janakpuri, New Delhi.

Infrastructure Excellence

An Ecosystem Engineered For Growth, Safety & Inspiration

At DPS Genius, we firmly believe that physical architecture and instructional environments profoundly influence a student's cognitive and emotional development. Our campus at C-58 Shahpuri Tower, Janakpuri is designed to international health and education standards.

Every space—from our vibrant digital smart classrooms and high-performance STEM laboratories to our hygienic dining spaces and monitored transport—is maintained to ensure safety, ergonomic comfort, and joyful academic immersion.

Strict Safety Audits

Fire certified, 100% CCTV coverage, and secure entry gates.

Comprehensive Amenities

Our Confirmed Campus Facilities

Verified infrastructure providing children with enriching scholastic, physical, and creative resources.

Interactive Smart Classrooms

Every classroom is equipped with high-resolution interactive touch panels, rich multimedia syllabus content, surround audio, and ergonomic student seating to make lessons vivid and participatory.

Computer & Coding Laboratory

Modern computing stations with optical fiber connectivity, child-safe firewalls, programming software (Python, Scratch, Web Design), and robotics kits that prepare students for technological literacy.

Composite Science Laboratory

Dedicated workstations equipped with compound microscopes, precision balances, chemical apparatus, biological specimens, and safety eye-wash stations meeting rigorous CBSE secondary requirements.

Learning Resource Center & Library

A quiet, sunlit haven housing thousands of fiction, non-fiction, encyclopedic, and reference books alongside leading educational periodicals to cultivate an enduring love for reading.

Sports Complex & Playgrounds

Outdoor playing grounds for athletics, cricket nets, football, basketball court, and indoor facilities for table tennis, chess, and yoga guided by licensed physical education instructors.

Safe GPS-Enabled Transportation

Air-conditioned and comfortable buses fitted with GPS live location tracking, certified speed governors, first aid units, and verified female attendants on every operational West Delhi route.

24x7 CCTV Campus Security

Complete camera coverage across corridors, gates, stairways, and activity zones monitored continuously from a centralized security station with visitor verification protocols.

Purified RO Drinking Water

Multi-stage industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) purification systems with UV sterilization and chilled water coolers tested periodically by certified municipal laboratories.

Hygienic & Sanitized Washrooms

Dedicated, gender-separated washrooms for primary, middle, and senior students, disinfected regularly throughout the day by trained housekeeping staff to ensure utmost cleanliness.

Medical Room & First Aid

A well-equipped infirmary supervised by a registered healthcare professional to provide immediate medical attention, basic emergency treatment, and routine health screenings.

Connectivity

Verified Transportation Coverage In West Delhi

Convenient pickup and drop-off points coordinated safely across major surrounding residential sectors.

Route 1: Janakpuri & Vikaspuri

Covers Blocks A, B, C, D of Janakpuri, District Center, Posangipur, Vikaspuri Blocks A-M, and Outer Ring Road junctions.

Route 2: Tilak Nagar & Subhash Nagar

Serving Tilak Nagar Metro corridor, Ashok Nagar, Fateh Nagar, Subhash Nagar, and Tagore Garden residential pockets.

Route 3: Uttam Nagar & Dwarka Mor

Connecting Uttam Nagar East/West, Mohan Garden, Om Vihar, Nawada, and Dwarka Mor arterial routes with timely service.
