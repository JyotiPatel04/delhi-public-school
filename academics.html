Academic Excellence (Class 1 to 10)

Empowering young intellects through experiential pedagogy, STEM inquiry, and conceptual clarity.

Pedagogical Framework

Nurturing Critical Thinkers & Creative Problem Solvers

At DPS Genius, academic design is rooted in the belief that each developmental stage from Class 1 to Class 10 demands carefully customized instruction. Our curriculum integrates national CBSE objectives with contemporary global learning benchmarks and the progressive directives of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).

Rather than relying on passive listening, our classrooms buzz with collaborative inquiry, laboratory experiments, interactive digital modules, and regular stage discussions. We ensure that students master foundational numeracy and language skills in the early grades, develop analytical rigor in middle school, and demonstrate exceptional board preparedness in secondary years.

Joyful Learning

Activity-based conceptual foundation for Class 1 to 5.

Foundational Stage

Primary Wing (Classes 1 to 5)

The primary years are a period of boundless curiosity and rapid cognitive growth. Our primary program focuses on joyful discovery, strong linguistic fundamentals, and concrete mathematical understanding.

Key Academic Focus Areas:

  • Language & Phonics: English & Hindi communication, reading comprehension circles, and expressive creative writing.
  • Experiential Mathematics: Visual and tactile number concepts, pattern recognition, and real-life problem solving.
  • Environmental Studies (EVS): Connecting classroom lessons to the natural world, plants, weather, communities, and eco-conservation.
  • Aesthetics & Fine Motor Development: Drawing, pottery, rhythmic music, and collaborative storytelling.

STEM & Logic

Analytical science, coding and practical research for Class 6 to 8.

Preparatory & Middle Stage

Middle School Wing (Classes 6 to 8)

As students transition from concrete understanding to abstract logic, the Middle School program introduces specialized scientific inquiry, algebraic systems, and deeper historical analysis.

Key Academic Focus Areas:

  • Separation of Sciences: Formal introduction to Physics, Chemistry, and Biology supported by hands-on lab experiments.
  • Advanced Mathematics: Algebraic equations, geometric proofs, data handling, and algorithmic deduction.
  • Social Sciences & Civics: Ancient and medieval civilizations, physical geography, constitutional frameworks, and civic responsibilities.
  • Computer Science & Coding: Logic building, flowchart design, Python/Scratch fundamentals, and digital ethics.
  • Third Language: Sanskrit / French / regional language foundations.

Board Readiness

CBSE curriculum mastery and career orientation for Class 9 & 10.

Secondary Stage

Secondary School Wing (Classes 9 & 10)

Our secondary curriculum prepares students for the CBSE All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE). We combine rigorous academic coaching with diagnostic testing, individualized remedial mentorship, and stress-free study strategies.

Key Academic Focus Areas:

  • CBSE Core Subjects: English Language & Literature, Mathematics (Standard & Basic), Science (Theory & Practical Lab), Social Science, and Second Language (Hindi / Sanskrit).
  • Skill Elective: Information Technology (IT 402) / Artificial Intelligence basics.
  • Mock Board Series: Time-management drills, structured model answer evaluations, and personalized teacher feedback.
  • Aptitude & Career Mentorship: Guiding students to understand their innate strengths for senior secondary stream selection.
