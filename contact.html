Campus Location & Accessibility

DPS Genius is centrally situated in the prime educational corridor of Janakpuri, West Delhi at Shahpuri Tower, offering easy road and metro connectivity.

How To Reach Our Campus:

  • By Delhi Metro: Blue Line: Janakpuri East or Janakpuri West Metro Station (approx. 5 minutes via e-rickshaw or cab). Magenta Line: Janakpuri West interchange.
  • By Road / DTC Bus: Close proximity to District Center Janakpuri and Outer Ring Road bus depots.
  • Visitor Parking: Dedicated visitor parking available at Shahpuri Tower commercial quadrangle.
