Our Inspiring Faculty & Mentors

Distinguished educators committed to lighting the spark of intellectual brilliance in every child from Class 1 to Class 10.

Mentorship That Inspires

Teachers Who Believe In Every Student's Potential

At DPS Genius, our educators are more than teachers—they are academic mentors, compassionate guides, and role models. Every member of our teaching community is thoroughly qualified, background verified, and regularly upskilled in child psychology, smartboard interactive software, and progressive NEP 2020 pedagogical approaches.

With an enviable 15:1 student-to-teacher ratio, our faculty ensures that no child is lost in the crowd. From early reading foundations in Class 1 to advanced board exam strategies in Class 10, our educators dedicate themselves wholeheartedly to each child's success.
