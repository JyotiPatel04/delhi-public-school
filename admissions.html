Documentation

Required Documents For Admission

Please ensure the following self-attested documents are available at the time of formal enrollment.

Birth Certificate

Official birth certificate issued by the Municipal Corporation / Competent Government Authority (Original for verification + 1 photocopy).

Previous Report Cards

Photocopy of the academic report card from the previous school for candidates seeking admission to Class 2 and above.

Transfer Certificate (TC)

Countersigned Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous recognized school (Mandatory for Class 2 to 10 admissions).

Address & Identity Proof

Aadhaar Card of the student, alongside Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID / Utility Bill of parent/guardian as residence proof.

Passport Photographs

5 recent passport-sized color photographs of the student and 2 passport-sized photographs of each parent/guardian.

Medical Fitness & Blood Group

Basic medical fitness certificate from a registered medical practitioner with documented blood group record and allergy notes.

Fee Structure

Transparent, Value-Based Fee Policy

At DPS Genius, we believe high-caliber education must remain transparent, accessible, and free from hidden charges. Our fee structure is carefully structured to reflect the state-of-the-art facilities, smart classrooms, experienced faculty, and all-round developmental opportunities provided to every child.

  • Tuition Fee: Covers core academic instruction, digital smartboard learning, and co-curricular periods.
  • Activity & STEM Fee: Covers science laboratory consumables, computer lab access, robotics workshops, and cultural events.
  • Transport Fee: Optional; zoned according to distance from Shahpuri Tower, Janakpuri with dedicated GPS buses.
  • Payment Convenience: Quarterly installment options via net banking, UPI, cards, or demand drafts.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, classes, timings, and procedures.

What classes are offered at DPS Genius?
DPS Genius provides complete schooling from Class 1 through Class 10, divided into the Primary Wing (Class 1-5), Middle School (Class 6-8), and Secondary Wing (Class 9-10).
Which curriculum does the school follow?
The school follows a curriculum aligned with Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) guidelines, structured in accordance with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), emphasizing experiential learning, STEM inquiry, and holistic evaluation.
What are the school timings for students?
Primary Wing (Class 1 to 5) timings are from 8:15 AM to 1:45 PM. Middle and Secondary Wing (Class 6 to 10) timings are from 8:00 AM to 2:15 PM, Monday through Friday. Select co-curricular clubs and remedial guidance sessions are held on Saturdays.
Is safe school bus transportation available across West Delhi?
Yes, our dedicated fleet of GPS-monitored buses serves Janakpuri, Vikaspuri, Tilak Nagar, Uttam Nagar, Dwarka, Subhash Nagar, and adjacent West Delhi neighborhoods. Each bus is accompanied by a verified driver, speed governor, first-aid kit, and caring female conductor.
Is an entrance test required for admission?
For Primary Wing (Class 1 to 5), admissions involve a friendly informal interaction to gauge developmental milestones. For Class 6 to 10, an age-appropriate diagnostic assessment in English, Mathematics, and Science is conducted to understand the student's foundation and customize subsequent academic mentoring.
