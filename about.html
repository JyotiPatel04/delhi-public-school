Our Origin & Journey

An Institution Dedicated to Formative Brilliance

Established with an earnest vision to redefine foundational and secondary education in West Delhi, DPS Genius (Delhi Play School) has emerged as an esteemed center of scholastic and personal development for students from Class 1 to Class 10.

Situated at C-58 Shahpuri Tower, Janakpuri, our campus offers a sanctuary of focused learning amidst the bustling metropolis of New Delhi. We recognize that education is not about rote memorization; it is about awakening the dormant genius within each child. By weaving foundational academic competencies with modern technology, robotics, sports, and classical ethics, we ensure our students stand tall as confident, responsible global citizens.

Over the years, DPS Genius has nurtured hundreds of young minds who continually demonstrate outstanding proficiency in competitive olympiads, inter-school cultural festivals, and board-level academic achievements.

Pedagogy

Our Educational Philosophy: "Foundation For The Future"

How we implement modern child-centric pedagogy aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020).

Our core philosophy revolves around the profound truth that a strong foundation in the early and middle school years determines a student's lifelong trajectory. Hence, our motto: "Foundation for the Future."

  • Experiential Learning: Concepts are not just read from textbooks; they are discovered in laboratories, manipulated through digital models, and tested in practical scenarios.
  • Multidisciplinary Thinking: We encourage students to see how science intersects with art, how mathematics governs music, and how history informs environmental responsibility.
  • Emotional & Social Well-Being: True education cultivates kindness, self-regulation, resilience in the face of setbacks, and collaborative spirit.
  • Continuous Assessment: Moving away from high-stakes rote examinations toward ongoing formative feedback that celebrates mastery and identifies areas for growth.
