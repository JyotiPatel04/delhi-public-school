About Our Institution

A Heritage of Academic Rigor & Character Building

Located conveniently at C-58 Shahpuri Tower, Janakpuri, New Delhi, DPS Genius (Delhi Play School) is a distinguished co-educational school dedicated to fostering intellectual curiosity, personal integrity, and a lifelong love for learning.

Our motto, "Foundation for the Future," reflects our deep commitment to sculpting resilient, creative, and well-rounded students from Class 1 through Class 10. Guided by the visionary National Education Policy (NEP) guidelines and an enriched CBSE-oriented curriculum, we ensure that every child thrives in academics, sports, and cultural arts.

  • Integrated STEM & Digital Learning: Hands-on robotics, coding fundamentals, and science labs.
  • Individual Mentorship: Low student-to-teacher ratio guaranteeing focused care.
  • Value-Driven Education: Instilling empathy, discipline, leadership, and ethical citizenship.

Admission Process: 5 Easy Steps

We have designed our admissions journey to be seamless, welcoming, and transparent for parents.

01

Submit Online Enquiry

Fill out our simple online admission enquiry form or connect via phone or WhatsApp with our admission counselors.

02

Campus Visit & Counseling

Visit our campus at Shahpuri Tower, Janakpuri to tour the smart classrooms, labs, and interact with academic coordinators.

03

Friendly Student Interaction

An informal, age-appropriate conversation to gauge student readiness, interests, and developmental milestones.

04

Document Verification

Submission of student birth certificate, previous school transfer certificate, report card, and residential proof.

05

Welcome to DPS Genius

Confirmation of enrollment, fee deposit, kit distribution, and student orientation into the DPS Genius family.
